What to ask and prepare before the visit

When you call, share the address, a good callback number, and a plain description of the issue. If you know where the main water shutoff is, mention that too. You can ask what kind of assessment the problem may require and how pricing will be explained before work begins.

A quick checklist can make the conversation easier:
- Identify the affected fixture, room, or part of the property.
- Note whether the issue is constant, intermittent, or getting worse.
- Keep the area around the problem accessible if it is safe to do so.
- Mention any previous repairs or unusual changes you have noticed.

Avoid pouring chemical drain products into a drain as a first response, especially if you do not know what is causing the blockage. Don’t open walls or attempt electrical work around a water heater. If water is spreading or a leak seems serious, call for professional help rather than waiting for it to become more disruptive.
